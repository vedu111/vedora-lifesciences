Restore — Free Physical Therapy & Physiotherapy Clinic Template

Restore is a free, two-page website template for physical therapy, physiotherapy and rehab clinics, built on Bootstrap 5. It includes a conversion-focused home page (hero appointment mini-form, services grid, therapist profiles, a four-step recovery-journey timeline, testimonials slider, insurance strip, FAQ and hours/location) plus a detailed Treatments & Booking page with filterable treatment cards, transparent pricing and a full booking form. The design uses a calm deep-teal-and-coral palette with a Hanken Grotesk / Mulish type pairing, is fully responsive with a working mobile menu, and is free for both personal and commercial use. It is completely jQuery-free — the mobile nav, scrollspy, count-ups, testimonial slider, treatment filtering and form validation are all plain vanilla JavaScript, built with progressive enhancement so content stays visible with JavaScript disabled. The contact and booking forms are front-end only; point their action at your backend or a form service to make them live. See https://uicookies.com/license/ for license details.
